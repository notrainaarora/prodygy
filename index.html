Move to bend the field

Run by a high school founder, for high school founders.

Most high schoolers with a business idea have nowhere to take it. Class projects end with the semester, competitions end at the pitch, and the people who have built companies are hard to reach at 16. Prodygy exists to change that.

Built by students

An app, a service, a tool or a business, treated as a company with real users.

Built with mentors

Each founder is paired with a mentor from our board and meets with them a couple of times a month.

Built together

A community of high schoolers trading feedback and pushing each other to finish.

How it works

  • Weekly updatesEvery founder posts a short update each week: what they shipped, what they learned, what is next. The whole cohort sees them.Required
  • Speaker sessionsA weekly session with a founder, operator or investor who has built something real.Weekly
  • MentoringOne-on-one time with a mentor from our board, plus group office hours.Twice a month
  • Demo DayFounders pitch the company they have built to investors, mentors and guests at a live event with speakers.End of summer

Where we are

Founded in September 2026. We are choosing a founding cohort of 10 to 15 high school founders who build through the year and pitch at Demo Day, planned for the end of summer 2027. The program is free, and founders keep 100% of their companies.
